why this one

  • price: below every row in the comparison, including the marketplace floor
  • no contract, no minimum, no platform to migrate off
  • the free test needs no credit card and no signup
  • I set the machine up ahead of time with vLLM or Ollama installed, ready to run

  • if the machine cuts out mid-job, those hours are on me
  • send me your workload and I will come back within a day with a cost estimate against your current cloud
  • one person runs it. You email me and I answer, no ticket queue
  • GPU hours and an OpenAI-compatible API from the same small supplier

What you give up: no SLA, no big-cloud compliance paperwork, capacity for a few teams only. If you need an SLA today, use a big cloud.
